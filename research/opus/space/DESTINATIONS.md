## Flags / could not verify
- **Gauntlet, Thule, Hunt The THING:** each has an official note that it was disabled, replaced or deactivated. I found nothing later that says any of them is back.
- **Erebos, Athena, Hermes and Zeus stations:** listed only on an undated fan map.
- **Exodus Space Station:** appears only in story text.
- **F.O.M.A., Arkadia Moon, Arkadia Underground and DSEC-9:** current only through player evidence. I found no official 2026 note for any of them.
- **Crystal Palace:** the newest official evidence is from Jun 2025, so it may be stale.
- **Unreal Engine switch:** MindArk plans the Unreal Engine 5 client "before Christmas 2026". The destination list and travel rules should be checked again after the switch.
