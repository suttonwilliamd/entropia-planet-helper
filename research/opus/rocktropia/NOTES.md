## Key findings

- **MindArk ownership.** MindArk took over ROCKtropia from NEVERDIE on 2024-06-10 (press release). Deeds went on the Exchange on 2024-07-02 (18.4.2). NEVERDIE now runs Howling Mine.
- **Biggest change: 19.12.0 (2026-11-11).** RT got a Codex for all regular creature groups. *New hunting mission chains can no longer be started.* Chains already started can be finished for 6 months; after that you can only finish your current stage. This makes most of the old guide data (the ~30 per-creature kill-point chains, the Hell chains, the Secret Island gorilla chain) unusable for new players. The notes do not say whether the story chains (Boot Camp, National Guard, Police Academy, Prison) count as "hunting mission chains". I flagged each of them with a warning rather than guessing.
- **19.12.0 also:** claimable plots near Club NEVERDIE, with plot tokens dropping from creature loot anywhere on RT; creature renames (Horny Little Devil to Happy Little Devil, Sissy to Prison Rookie, Bootylicious to Showstopper, Voluptuous to Glamorous); Vixens moved to the hills; Haters buffed.
- **19.12.1 (2026-11-17):** Shy Vixens made beginner-friendly again; Hater damage reduced slightly; Codex challenges added (Forum Trolls, Street Life, Hater progression, Panther Puny, Old Harlem Thugs and Bonzo); AI spawns restored so The AI War can progress.
- **Main daily:** The AI War. Kill 40 AI; weapon and Cheap A$$ Ammo supplied; rewards are a 2 PED Presidential credit card, the ammo and a pay stub. Wiki says "repeats daily"; rocktropia.com says "resets every 24 hours". I encoded it as rolling 24 h.
- **Police calls** need the Police Radio from Police Academy III. Police Call 10-57 is "repeatable every 24 hours" (EntropiaCentral). Call rewards are not documented anywhere I found.
- **Getting there:** Official-forum threads (Dec 2025 to Mar 2026) show Setesh exits to Calypso. rocktropia.com claims a shuttle from Setesh straight to RT, but I could not confirm that, so it is not stated as fact. Teleportation Tokens cost 10 per planet trip (Dec 2025; launched at 15) with a 150 kg limit. 18.15.1 (2025-06-17) listed RT token travel as a known issue. The Oct 2026 event post says tokens work, but I found no official "fixed" note.
- **Hell access:** free via the teleporter drop-down from RT or Secret Island (2026-02-01 player post; also used for the Oct 2026 event). The old item text for the Telemans Coin says it pays passage to Hell. That looks historical; the coin is now a Hell Key crafting input.

## Contradictions and stale info

- AI War giver: "Presidential Recruiter" (Daily Entropia 2026, entropiawiki) vs "Sanctioned Weapons Dealer" (joinentropia, EntropiaCentral location table). Same waypoint in both. Rewards also listed differently (Cheap A$$ Ammo "1 PED TT" vs "10000 Cheap A$$ Ammo").
- Police Academy giver: EntropiaCentral overview says Lt. Funk gives all three; its PA III page names Cpt. Shramsky.
- Hell waypoints: joinentropia writes Hell coordinates with planet "Rocktropia". Nexus and event posts use "HELL"/"Hell". I only used HELL/Hell waypoints as verified and put the others in label text.
- Secret Island return: one fan page says you must wait an hour before using the return agent; the 2022 blog puts the 1-hour limit on the outbound agent. Both are noted.
- Shy Vixen waypoint 136305, 85040 (old parking lot) is treated as historical, per the build lead's acceptance note. It is in the label text only, with waypoint null and verified false, because 19.12.0 moved the Vixens.
- The Entropia Codex entry-level page says RT has no Codex. That was true before 19.12.0 and is now outdated.
- joinentropia and Entropia Nexus maps still list Hunt The THING. rocktropia.com "features THE THING". All stale for the instance.
- All the mission reward numbers trace back to Fyrs's 2016 "Consolidated Rocktropia Guide and info" thread. Copies of it were updated in 2026 (EntropiaCentral, 2026-08-13), but nobody re-checked the rewards in game. I marked these medium or low confidence.
- The Enjar Hell post is undated. Sister posts on Hive are dated 2022-09-25/26, so I treat it as about 2022 and possibly stale.
