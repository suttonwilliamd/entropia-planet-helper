# Toulan

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Virtualsense's planet shares Elder Tribute and crossover progression with DSEC-9. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **Arrive at the Citadel.** Take a shared warp to Toulan Space Station, `/wp [Space, 85276, 81077, -428, Toulan Space Station]`, and the station-to-planet TP. Yamato lists Toulan legs and 6 PED per schedule for paying passengers. Qualifying born avatars, estate owners and Toulan citizens can qualify for free travel. The November 2026 operator notice confirms event-weekend schedule shifts. Kronan lists Toulan as a request stop. MindArk 18.9 established a 150 kg cap and removed the ordinary station-to-own-planet fee. 19.2.0 reduced published fares to 6 Teleportation Tokens station-to-station and 10 planet-to-planet for eligible links. Confirm the actual menu before buying tokens. [^T23] Store valuable lootables before personal flight through lootable PvP. [^T1] [^T2] [^T3]
2. **Open visitor rewards.** At the foot of the Citadel TP stairs take **Ahlan's Wedding Ring**, `/wp [Planet Toulan, 132641, 94073, 280, Ahlan]`. Swim to the marker behind the tower `/wp [Planet Toulan, 132645, 93996, 254, Ring]`. Proximity triggers the find. Return for **Ahlan's Old Vehicle (L)**. Start the public storyline at Senan in Al-Nahar City `/wp [Planet Toulan, 134489, 96469, 274, Senan]`, then Rabee at the Nawa Reactor `/wp [Planet Toulan, 134055, 96748, 323, Rabee]` for the pipe route. Older retrospective notes mention Salam/Bilal for visitors. The official born guide explicitly restricts its extended starter chain; verify eligibility before promising offworld pistol and outfit rewards. [^T4] [^T5]
3. **Born avatars.** Toulan- and Monrian-born follow the eligible chain from Salam `/wp [Planet Toulan, 132665, 94190, 271, Salam]` through Bilal and Ahlan to Guardian Village. Follow the partner's 20-step route for starter tools, weapon/ammo and clothing. The Toulan-born shirt is origin-specific. 25.2.1 lets qualifying born avatars bypass the **Where's My Pants** riddle at Nadira and get the waypoint. The born guide flags that mission loot may fail in a team. [^T4] [^T6]
4. **NawaCore MK1 → hunting/instances.** Visitors ask the NawaCore Agent at Nahar City's gate and choose between hunting **100 TabTab** or handing in **10,000 Vibrant Sweat**. Compare the sweat cost with a hunt and take the kill option if already hunting TabTab. Toulan-born receive MK1 on leaving the start room. For MK2 then MK3 bring the prior core and the next core's A–E parts, dropped in CUHOF or traded by players. Check which core tier is required for each intended mission. [^T7]
5. **Two longer goals.** For **Toulan Citizenship** bring MK3 to Haneen at the Citadel info booth `/wp [Planet Toulan, 132614, 94097, 280, Haneen]`. Progress comes from qualifying monthly event mobs. The guide reports shared-loot kills outside formal teams counting, usually across several events. The practical rewards are Yamato eligibility and the outfit. For **Worship the Elder Gods**, go from Pit North TP to `/wp [Planet Toulan, 137269, 92209, 119, Church]`; hand-ins start at the nearby Shrine `/wp [Planet Toulan, 137257, 92168, 114, Shrine]`. Unlock it early if already mining or hunting eligible mobs. [^T1] [^T8] [^T9]

## Spend or carry and optional branches

- **Mirsal Tokens** buy from the Citadel or Nahar Towers trader. Partner VUs added weapon/tool blueprints and CUHOF Legends key blueprints. Estate Lost Pages are an optional expense. Save for a specific item and verify live stock, transport flags and ingredient costs. [^T15] [^T16] [^T17]
- **Nawa materials:** Ordinary CUHOF/Mini entry uses Nawa Energy Cells. Refine 100 Vibrant Sweat with one Nawa Shard (from two Nawa Fragments) per cell. Check the entry count at the portal. **CUHOF Legends** uses separate solo/team keys crafted from limited blueprints. 25.1 states ordinary CUHOF 1–5 end chests were removed in favor of creature loot. Legends has its own two-part run and chest stage. [^T7] [^T16]
- **Elder Tributes:** Keep enough in inventory for the next robe threshold before buying trader items. The Toulan and DSEC-9 Churches share some assignments. See DSEC-9 for full thresholds and the Silver Crown of Sahar crossover. [^T9] [^T13] [^T14]
- **Gear:** 25.1 added Improved Baraka, 25.3 added Modified Baraka, and 26.1 added component and weapon blueprints. 26.3 added CUHOF armor plate upgrades II–V. **26.4 (2026-11-11)** grants them 0.5%, 1%, 1.5% and 2% block chance respectively. Virtualsense says the UI may truncate percentages. Ask the CUHOF Caves merchant for current requirements. [^T16] [^T18] [^T19] [^T20] [^T21]
