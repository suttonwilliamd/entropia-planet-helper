# Next Island and Ancient Greece

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Next Island is a planet and Ancient Greece is its linked time-travel area with a separate map. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **Arrive at Next Island.** Use a shared warp when fares and return timing suit the visit. Virtualsense lists Next Island on its Yamato route, with 6 PED for a paying passenger per schedule and November 2026 event-specific shifts. Kronan lists Next Island as a request stop. Use the ship operator's current boarding/cargo instructions. Alternatively, the published 19.2.0 fares are 6 Teleportation Tokens station-to-station and 10 planet-to-planet on eligible links. 18.9 introduced the 150 kg cap and removed the ordinary station-to-own-planet fee, superseding the 7 PED in the older NI Academy page. [^N23] Leave valuable lootables in storage for flights through lootable PvP. [^N1] [^N2] [^N3] [^N4] [^N5]
2. **Collect starter gear and transport.** Go to the starter buildings down at the beach at Paradise Landing. The welcome route offers hunting, mining and crafting missions with gun/ammo, finder/probes/refiner and healing tool. The partner moved the new-avatar spawn down to the starter area in 18.5 and made the refiner untradeable. The older path down from the Paradise Landing TP remains a usable route from that TP. Do the roof chef's gathering mission if convenient. On to Crystal Peak, then north past L.A.R.A. and C.A.R.A. to the Cartographer and **Visit Resa Docks** for the Strider Mk.I (L). Take it to the black track near Crystal Peak for the driving mission before using it around aggro. Sharks are present offshore. [^N6] [^N7] [^N8]
3. **Time travel → Falcata.** Download Ancient Greece. At Orpheus' Cave TP `/wp [Next Island, 136178, 87223, 101, Orpheus' Cave]`, use the destination dropdown in Medium/Large TP view. NI Helper says the cave TP is pre-added on entry. Its current documented route uses the TP interface, so do not stockpile legacy time-travel crystals for this transfer. On the Greece side start **Meeting the Gods**, speak twice to Penthesilea `/wp [Ancient Greece, 36258, 20524, 146, Penthesilea]` before Creon `/wp [Ancient Greece, 36312, 20686, 186, Creon]`, then **Talk with the Elder** and **Looking at Distant Pasts** for the unlimited Falcata. [^N9] [^N10] [^N11]
4. **Unlock repeatable hubs before longer tracks.** Accept Crystal Hoarder on Next Island before the Greece trip. Thebes is the Greek market hub and quest center. Pick up the **Becoming an Islander** track there if planning extended visits. The Aug 2026 guide lists 137 Daily Islander Pursuit completions, with multiple activities to choose from and a reward bundle including the Islander Bumpercar and Giant L.A.R.A. Plushie. The partner opened the quest to both offworlders and True Islanders. [^N7] [^N12] [^N13]

## Spend or carry and optional long tracks

- **Elysian Tech Chips and crystals** are materials. Reserve chips for N.E.O. access or crafting and crystal rewards for planned recipes or trade. Match the Crystal Hoarder reward to the recipe or current market when choosing. Blueprint drops and chips are a different decision from taking ammo. [^N12] [^N18]
- **Nots and key fragments** are separate Thebes progression resources. Save Nots for a chosen terminal reward, which can require an extra item at full TT. Early purchases such as paintings and textures compete with saving for buff clothing or the Falcata-related exchange. Convert key fragments when intentionally running a dungeon. Cargo classification and current vendor costs need live verification. [^N15] [^N16] [^N19]
- **VIP/SVIP.** The shortest documented VIP gate is Haruspex at Flora in Tanzanite View. Attempt each appropriate non-colorable faction garment three times using the Multi-Faction Daily blueprints. Resa at Glenn in Kamba Outpost starts after Crystal Hoarder and requires 2,000 harvest points, 300 fruit, 100 stones, 50 dung and 5 PED TT of refining. Stel's 500 shirt attempts and 1,000 exact-name Island Sharks plus mining take longer. N.E.O. consumes crafted pieces and chips. Loyalist Peyton checks a mission record, and the community guide is uncertain about the full qualifying list. Keep unique blueprints while following any of these routes. All five VIPs plus Islander status open the later Thebes SVIP area. Magical Flowers gates an additional SVIP task. [^N15] [^N16] [^N18]
- **Gorgon branches.** For the older wave, **Deliver Us from the Dark Queen** gates entry. Follow the key assembly guide before going to Knossos. The first Shade spawn requires crafting, mining and hunting hand-ins with sweat. Later spawns use sweat alone per NI Helper. The 18.12.0 Thebes expansion added a separate dungeon/key-fragment route and Aegis of the Gorgonforged upgrade terminal. Read the entry and upgrade costs before gearing for a wave or dungeon.
