# Arkadia

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Scope covers the planet, Arkadia Underground and Arkadia Moon. The main player route uses Arkadia Archive pages updated in August 2026 and official notes where available. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **Arrive on Arkadia.** Shared warp to Arkadia Space Station, then take the station-to-planet teleporter. Kronan publishes Calypso–Arkadia service and boarding at Celeste Quarry or Twin Peaks. Ask the operator for the current fare and cargo rules. Interplanetary token travel has a 150 kg cap under 18.9. MindArk 19.2.0 reduced the published fare to 6 Teleportation Tokens station-to-station and 10 planet-to-planet on eligible links. The ordinary station-to-own-planet fee was removed in 18.9. [^A30] For personal flight, use a thruster and leave lootable materials in storage. Check the waypoint and PvP map before traveling to the Moon. [^A1] [^A2] [^A3]
2. **Passport → starter kit → puny islands.** Offworld visitors take **Arkadia Passport Verification** at the New Arrivals Hangar, `/wp [Arkadia, 30302, 17951, 184, Passport Officer]`, and hand in 10,000 Vibrant Sweat. At IFN Supply Depot, speak to Advisor Nina `/wp [Arkadia, 30044, 17828, 180, Advisor Nina]` for **A New Home**. Visit the medical, weapon, armor and clothing stores to get training weapons, a FAP, mining starter tools, ammo/probes and the Exploration Hoverpod MkI (L). For **Islands Beyond the Horizon**, ask Nina then Commander Sertris `/wp [Arkadia, 30145, 17430, 180, Commander Sertris]` at Resolute Firebase. This opens four low-HP auto-loot hunting islands for Carabok, Gallard, Hornet and Monura. Take the bonus kill chain if offered. [^A4] [^A5] [^A6] [^A7]
3. **IFN or unlimited rifle.** Complete **Welcome to IFN Basic Training** and see Drill Sergeant Edmund Kinzel `/wp [Arkadia, 17217, 23069, 541, Kinzel]` to start **Oratan Payback**. For the shorter gear goal, ask Nina for **Arkadia Protection Duty**, fill the hunting progress meter and claim the unlimited Herman LAW-R Mk.1. Larger targets advance the meter faster. Compare the added costs with a longer low-maturity hunt. [^A8] [^A9] [^A10]
4. **Underground side trip.** At a surface TP select **Arkadia Underground** from the top dropdown, then its entry TP. Pick up the Oratan Insignia contract at `/wp [Arkadia Underground, 13482, 21769, 143, Mysterious Traveller]` and pair it with matching surface Oratan Payback where the objective counts. For Sal'Diresh Vaults, check key and treasure-map requirements. The 2025 patch moved management of Sal'Diresh key missions to Professor Felix Dorne in 8 Coins HQ. [^A6] [^A11] [^A12] [^A13]
5. **Moon and backpack.** The Moon is a separate space destination, `/wp [Space, 76592, 58324, -50, Arkadia Moon]`. Confirm a drop-off and return before carrying valuables. The 2018 launch reports about having to fly back were superseded by the Arkadia 16.1 patch, which enabled a Moon-to-Arkadia TP for 7 PED. Its current live fee and any reverse surface-to-Moon link need confirmation after later universe-wide fare changes. [^A30] [^A31] At Gamma Portal take Milo's extractor plans as listed below. For the Adventurer's Backpack, wear the qualifying Hazmat gear or Smuggler gear and see Laura `/wp [Arkadia Moon, 14349, 10937, 204, Laura]`. Collect seven meteorite samples at the waypoints in the mission guide. [^A3] [^A14] [^A15] [^A16]

## Spend or carry and optional long tracks

- **Arkadia Insignias:** Save for a targeted Lunar Plate upgrade, the Mysterious Merchant's particle blueprints, or Sicarius Nest entry. On the Moon, Kayla `/wp [Arkadia Moon, 12047, 11625, 170, Kayla]` at the Genetic Labs accepts a Pure Particle Sample Mk4 on first entry per the August 2026 guide. Repeat entry uses one Heavy Duty Particle or, for Smugglers, 5 PED of Arkoin. Check cost before entering. [^A18] [^A24]
- **Arkoins:** Separate from Insignias. The Arkoin Broker at IFN Supply Depot `/wp [Arkadia, 30008, 17877, 180, Arkoin Broker]` offers clothes, armor and BLP/plasma weapons. They also pay for optional secret camps and certain instance fees. Keep a planned entry budget and compare broker gear to owned gear. Verify cargo classification before transport. [^A9] [^A24] [^A25]
- **IFN Special Operations:** Captain Darius `/wp [Arkadia Moon, 11352, 13149, 350, Captain Darius]` begins recruitment. Train across all three regions to earn IFN Spec Ops armor pieces and access Sicarius Nest for upgrades. The player guide warns that instance kills fail to progress the Moon stage that says “any mob.” Treat this and the Smuggler laboratory as deliberate combat spending choices. [^A26]
- **Viceroy and keyed instances:** Armor hand-ins and upgraded versions are optional; check the piece and binding before surrendering materials. A May 2026 report says a partially completed Viceroy set failed to offer its next helmet mission. Use current offers and mission journal. Deeds are optional investments. [^A12] [^A14] [^A27] [^A28]

## Patch reconciliation and gaps

- April 2025 fixed Nina. The official remedy for a stale A New Home chain was abandon and retake it and abandon the old original starter quest if active to receive the hoverpod. Use this only for that documented stale-quest case. December 2025 added new stage 1–6 ACA lines. March 2026 added surface trophies/figurines and fixed waypoint, spawn-marker and instance issues. [^A12] [^A28] [^A29]
- The 2018 Moon launch thread gives historical entry conditions. Current Moon docking, local TP options, cargo rules and hunting/mining tax need field verification. Also check the Oratan and Insignia trigger clocks, and whether all passport/IFN gates are identical for returning and planet-born avatars. [^A4] [^A9] [^A14] [^A18]
