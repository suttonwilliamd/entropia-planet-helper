# DSEC-9

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Virtualsense's DSEC-9 is an outpost region reached through Monria. Elara and Mountain of Madness are DSEC-9 map regions. Toulan is the separate planet involved in the crossover. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **Monria → DSEC9.** Use Monria's arrival route and a local Monria TP. Select **DSEC9** in the map dropdown and use the DSEC9 entry teleporter. This is the documented local route and needs no standalone warp destination for DSEC-9. Bring a sweating tool, basic gear and a small ammo budget. Virtualsense reaffirmed the Mountain of Madness route in October 2026. [^D1] [^D2] [^D3]
2. **First priority, Mountain of Madness.** From entry reach **Elara Terminus** `/wp [DSEC9, 35957, 20010, 105, Elara Terminus]`. Follow the wall west to the pod `/wp [DSEC9, 35140, 20357, 104, Tele-pod to Elara]` and interact with its panel. In Elara capture the Lotus TP in the temple at `/wp [DSEC9, 34844, 22139, 99, Lotus Teleporter]`, then travel northeast to `/wp [DSEC9, 36428, 23447, 99, Mountain of Madness Device]`. Booting the radio towers and obtaining a laptop are separate from this transit. Once inside the Mountain, vehicles and teleport chips are disabled, so capture TPs on foot. [^D2]
3. **Beginner chain → repeatables.** Talk to the Stranger `/wp [DSEC9, 36754, 23522, 104, Stranger in the Woods]` for **Welcome to the Dark Side**. Gather ten mushrooms on the way to the Old Man `/wp [DSEC9, 37489, 23899, 108, Old Man]`, following the river to skirt aggro. Accept the hunt/sweat objective, return with ten sweat, then follow the mining and later hunting objectives. The first run gives starter equipment and ammo and opens **Clear the Path** and blueprint repeatables. Capture **Larches Green** `/wp [DSEC9, 38123, 23647, 100, Larches Green]` for the Church and repeat trips. Virtualsense added local trade, repair, storage, revival and a turret at `/wp [DSEC9, 37273, 23882, 101, Sweat Area Terminals]` near the Old Man. [^D2] [^D4] [^D5] [^D16]
4. **Valeria's access branch.** At the entry outpost see Valeria `/wp [DSEC9, 33280, 16852, 145, Valeria]`. Take the televator to the lookout on floor 2. Boot radio towers 1 and 2 and report to the Alpha and Alpha-2 outposts respectively. Boot towers 3 and 4, then step into the center of the Elara Terminus TP to trigger the update. Visit its outpost upstairs, then Pam in the Terminus building. Follow the OTIS reboot and Comms Terminal instructions, then follow Derelict to Steve the Engineer for the access card. Return to Derelict for Laptop 01 and entry to DEC's Holoroom. This opens the armor upgrade path for DSEC Basic Armor. [^D6] [^D7]
5. **Laptop → CorBite → Silver Crown.** In the Holoroom at `/wp [DSEC9, 36099, 20232, 104, Holoroom]` pick up Workstation 2, 3 and 4 objectives as each unlocks. Loot the 12TB Laptop HDD, Laptop RAM, then DSEC OS 2.0 while each mission is active. Turn each in to Derelict's cave `/wp [DSEC9, 35263, 19120, 152, Derelict]`. OTIS near Elara Terminus is a documented farming option; drops have no guaranteed time. Start the crossover at Workstation 4 and carry Laptop 04 and the Access Card when traveling to Rais at CorBite on Toulan. Follow the alternating DEC/Rais resource objectives and the OTIS Commander stage. The operator states Commander appears daily at a random time and remains until killed. Finish **Prove Your Worth**, 30 crafting assignments issued every 20 h. Missed days extend the run. The final NPC at Toulan's Narian Temple provides the **Silver Crown of Sahar** with a crafting buff. The early broken crown is a quest step. [^D7] [^D8] [^D9]

## Spend or carry and obsolete routes

- **Elder Tributes:** Carry in inventory when claiming the robe. Thresholds are 150, 420, 810, 1,320 and 1,980; Tributes roll forward and earlier missions stay available as new ones unlock. Retain enough for the next robe and stage before spending at the Elder Tribute Trader. Trader recipes can also charge PED. Lower robes are untradeable per the partner guide, while the top High Priest/Priestess robe is tradeable. [^D12]
- **Repair parts and gear:** Keep useful parts for Steve's exchanges, upgrades and crossover hand-ins. Virtualsense disabled the **two-hour picking-parts missions**. Parts now drop in normal loot without that timer, so older routes relying on timed picking fall out of date. Optional armor upgrades and crossover crafting should be budgeted separately from the low-cost newcomer loop. [^D6] [^D8] [^D14]
- **Long optional crossovers:** Carry the Access Card and Laptop in inventory for gated NPCs, and leave unnecessary lootables in storage for the Monria–Toulan space leg. Use Yamato's current price and dates. The November 2026 operator post confirms scheduled runs again after the September interruption. [^D1] [^D3] [^D8]
