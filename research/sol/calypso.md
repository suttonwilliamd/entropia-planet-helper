# Calypso system

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Scope includes Setesh, Crystal Palace and Asteroid F.O.M.A. The dates below describe public evidence. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **New avatar, Setesh.** The default onboarding is on **Moon Setesh in the Calypso system**. At Port Cabrakan start with Security Chief Harkov, then follow the tracked starter missions through hunting, gear and travel. Prioritize the Port Cabrakan mission for the **SET-P1 Civilian Sidearm, Adjusted** and **Recon In The Jungle** for the Vivo S10. MindArk moved Vivo S10 here from the Calypso Gauntlet and lowered the reward's starting TT value to 2 PED in 19.10.1. Some rewards are excluded for avatars that completed Thule. Check each reward before using any skip option. [^C1] [^C2] [^C3] [^C21] [^C22]
2. **Continue on Calypso.** Use the onboarding transport when offered. Returning players reported a Port Atlantis pilot down the stairs from the TP, just past the service center on the right. Android Cecilia supplied a waypoint to the pilot in March 2026. The same thread describes scrolling the Calypso Space Station TP list to Setesh. On a return visit, players reported the departure NPC no longer offering the trip. The skip terminal in the service building opposite the Cabrakan TP, `/wp [Setesh, 67634, 68354, 299, Skip Terminal]`, reportedly offers an exit after the tutorial. Setesh storage is separate. Verify repeat-trip access before storing gear or taking the return shuttle. [^C23] **Small Iron** from Paul Reinhardt unlocks after **Cliffchaser Run** under 19.12.0, including while remaining on Setesh. [^C2] [^C4] [^C5]
3. **Port Atlantis/Camp Icarus.** Pick up MindArk's November 2026 Port Atlantis newcomer chain about Argonauts, robots and the CDF, and collect relevant Daily Terminal assignments before killing. A Nov 24 player report flags missing Argonauts at the second Iron Road waypoint, with another player reporting progress after talking to the Argonaut Research Camp daily NPC. Treat that as a troubleshooting lead. 19.12.1 restored RDI Facility access near Fort Lahar for **Point of Origin**. For normal hunting, advance creature Codex alongside missions. Old Iron Challenge ladders were retired even though recent quests use “Iron” in their names. [^C5] [^C6] [^C7] [^C8]
4. **Interplanetary arrival.** A shared scheduled warp avoids buying a ship and thruster. Compare the live shared fare and boarding point with an existing ship's fuel/decay or station teleport cost. MindArk 18.9 introduced token travel with a 150 kg cap and removed the ordinary station-to-own-planet teleport fee. **19.2.0 reduced the published fare to 6 Teleportation Tokens station-to-station and 10 planet-to-planet** on eligible links. Check the actual TP listing, especially for moons. [^C21] A personal launch needs a thruster-equipped ship, and cargo should stay in storage if the route crosses lootable PvP. Both non-lootable PvP and safe zones also exist. Kronan posts scheduled Calypso/Arkadia legs and cargo handling. [^C9] [^C10] [^C11]

## Repeatables in recommended order

| ID | Mission and route | Requirement and reward | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cal-terminal-hunt | Roll the hunting category at a Daily Mission Terminal, ideally before any same-mob hunting. | Reported easy bonus variants are 10 Gibnib, Chirpy or Fugabarba for 20 Daily Tokens. Other rolls vary. Rarity and frequency are unconfirmed. | 16 h from acceptance according to a 2025 player report and guide. [^C12] [^C13] |
| cal-crystal-cave | Get the Crystals/Crystal Cave daily from the NPC at the Port Atlantis west entrance, enter Feffoid Cave, kill 15 Feffoids and interact with the crystal. | 1 Daily Token. Budget healing or repair based on your gear. | 20 h reported. Trigger unverified. [^C12] |
| cal-argonaut-cave | Argonaut cave NPC at `/wp [Calypso, 62681, 76112, 123, Argo daily]`. Kill 50 Argonaut Young. | 1 Daily Token. Can stack with Argonaut Codex. Iron stones can be refined for a crafting roll. More combat cost than the bird bonus. | 16 h from finish per Aug 2025 player report. [^C12] [^C13] |
| cal-terminal-craft | Roll crafting category before choosing a blueprint. | Bonus observed as 51 Basic Screws attempts, 1 Iron Ingot per click and 10 Daily Tokens. Stock iron only if you expect to use it. | Same 16 h acceptance timer reported for the terminal. [^C12] |
| cal-terminal-mine | Roll mining category before dropping probes. | Lysterium or Oil bonus observed at 25 PED extracted for 20 Daily Tokens. Probe spend and claim RNG make this optional for non-miners. | Same 16 h acceptance timer reported for the terminal. [^C12] |
| cal-trader-handoff | Trader Joanna at Camp Icarus and Joel at Cabrakan. | MindArk added item hand-ins in 19.0.0. Read the offered reward and compare item value before confirming. | Patch calls these dailies. Current reward pool or exact timer unverified. [^C1] |

**Spend or carry.** Keep Daily Tokens until the trader has an item you can use at your profession level. The documented inventory is largely limited gear, so compare the token price and remaining useful life before redeeming. Daily, Combat, Mayhem and Teleportation Tokens are separate. Monria also has a Daily Token Trader. Store lootable crafting materials rather than taking them through risky space just to use a trader. [^C10] [^C14] [^C15]

## Change notes and gaps

- 19.0.0 temporarily disabled the Gauntlet and relocated the Vivo S10. 19.2.0 rebuilt Port Atlantis, Camp Icarus and nearby beginner content. A later Gauntlet reactivation and exact Vivo/sidearm dialogue and waypoints remain unverified. Follow the current starter route in the quest tracker. [^C1] [^C5] [^C21] [^C22]
- The current Port Atlantis chain needs an in-game pass for its start NPC, route, rewards, and the Iron Road spawn report. The release note verifies the chain and the separate Small Iron gating change. [^C5] [^C6] [^C7]
- Verify Daily Terminal bonus RNG, merchant inventory/binding, and the stated timers by noting acceptance, completion and turn-in timestamps separately. [^C12] [^C14]
