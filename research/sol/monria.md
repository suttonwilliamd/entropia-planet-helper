# Monria

Desk-checked 2026-11-26. Virtualsense's moon is a separate space destination with a local teleporter link to DSEC-9. Field verification by this project is pending.

## Arrival and strongest unlocks

1. **Take a shared warp to Monria.** Virtualsense's Yamato operator lists 6 PED for paying passengers per schedule, with free eligibility for qualifying born avatars, estate owners and Toulan citizens. Its schedule starts/ends on Monria and usually runs Fridays and Sundays, shifting to Thursday/Monday around Toulan event weekends. September 2026 saw suspended runs; a November 2026 operator announcement again publishes dates. Confirm the current run, destination station, signup and summons close to departure. Kronan lists Monria as a request stop. A sourced space waypoint is `/wp [Space, 58054, 81246, -56, Monria Space Station]`. [^M1] [^M2] [^M3] [^M4]
2. **Protect cargo and plan the exit.** Use the operator's instructions and avoid carrying lootables in personal flight across lootable PvP. MindArk 18.9 made ordinary station-to-own-planet teleports free and established a 150 kg cap for token travel. 19.2.0 lowered the published fare to 6 Teleportation Tokens station-to-station and 10 planet-to-planet on eligible links. Confirm the actual Monria station/destination menu or the operator's drop instructions, since the generic patch does not specify every moon. [^M23] [^M1] [^M4] [^M5]
3. **Claim the local hubs, then pick complementary missions.** Monria Hub is for crafting and traders. At DSEC Forensics pick up the Shoggoth daily from Sachem Ursa, and start **The Order of the Elder Gods** at neighboring Pinthas `/wp [Monria, 38145, 21022, 11, Pinthas]`. Start **Toy Soldier** at the Monria Hub to gain plushie progress from the same hunts. Unlock the Mining Camp, Military Camp and Shub Cavern as your gear and activities allow. Use the directory for services and map/waypoint guide for routes. [^M6] [^M7] [^M8] [^M9]
4. **Start cross-region content via Monria's TPs.** In any Monria TP choose DSEC9 in the top-right map dropdown and select its entry TP. DSEC-9's **Welcome to the Dark Side** is available to visitors, with beginner gear and its own repeatables. Monrian-born avatars can also qualify for the Toulan starter chain according to Virtualsense. See the DSEC-9 and Toulan files for those routes. [^M10] [^M11]

## Spend or carry

- **Daily Tokens** can be held for an equipment choice at the Monria Hub second-floor token trader area, around `/wp [Monria, 38147, 22079, 212, Token Traders]`. Combat and Mayhem traders are separate. Confirm the current vendor stock and trade/binding rules. Delay spending until the limited item matches your profession level or planned activity. [^M18]
- **Horns of Z'agol** are the long-term combat reward from completing four chapters of the Order. The operator's guide documents 3% reload speed. Shoggoths, Yogs, Cultists and Shubs are the documented chapter sources in order, and the chapter drops are rare. Run this alongside the dailies. Older posts discussing tradeable chapter copies predate the change making the chapters untradeable. [^M7] [^M19] [^M20]
- **Crafting:** Count costs per success. Skills are a payoff, and 500 successes may take far more than 500 attempts. The 2025 player discussion supports the crafting and mining daily routes being in use but does not resolve the clock discrepancy. Compare the separate Toulan route if you already plan to craft there. [^M16] [^M21]

## Change notes and gaps

- The October 2026 DSEC-9 event instructions still use the Monria connection, and Virtualsense announced November Yamato runs. Verify any route changes, station-to-Monria options and exact costs at booking. [^M1] [^M2] [^M5] [^M10]
- Some published NPC coordinates differ by a few units or by floor, notably Tobie and Angel. Use the second-floor Trade Center direction and current NPC name over stale altitude. Curd's old 4.2 token issue is historical; the later directory still lists the daily and the dedicated thread lists the reward. Exact contemporary counts, award behavior and craft auto-counting need a field pass. [^M6] [^M7] [^M13] [^M15] [^M16] [^M22]
